⭐ Week 2
Sitting Like a Superhero! 🦸
Did you know that how you sit at the computer is very important? Sitting correctly keeps your back healthy and your eyes safe. Let's learn how to sit like a superhero!
📖 Let's Learn
The Perfect Computer Posture
1
Sit up straight! Keep your back touching the back of the chair. Imagine there is a string pulling the top of your head up! ⬆️
2
Feet flat on the floor — both feet touch the ground. No dangling! 🦶
3
Eyes level with the screen — the screen should be straight in front of you, not too high, not too low. 👀
4
Arms relaxed — let your elbows bend softly. Your hands rest gently on the table. 🤲

💡 The Magic Power Button
How to Turn On a Computer
1
Find the Power Button ⏻ on the computer case or screen. It usually has this symbol: ⏻
2
Press it once — gently! Don't hold it for too long.
3
Wait patiently 🕐 — the computer needs a moment to wake up, just like you in the morning! 😴➡️😀
4
When you see the desktop (the main screen), the computer is ready! 🎉
🖱️ Week 2
Hello Mouse! Our Digital Hand! 👋
The mouse is one of the most important computer tools. It is like your hand inside the computer! When you move the mouse, an arrow moves on the screen. Let's learn how to hold it!
📖 How to Hold the Mouse
The Right Way to Hold Your Mouse 🖱️
1
Place your palm gently on top of the mouse. It should feel comfortable, like holding a little pet! 🐭
2
Your index finger (pointing finger) rests on the left button. This is the button you click most! 👆
3
Your thumb rests on the left side. Your other fingers rest gently on the right side. 🤏
4
Move the mouse slowly and smoothly — don't lift it in the air! Slide it on the mouse pad. 🎯
🎮 Memory Game
Mouse Parts — Tap to Reveal! 🕵️
👆 What does the LEFT button do? (tap to find out!)
🖱️ The LEFT button is for clicking! You use it to select things, open files, and play games. It's the most important button!
👆 What is the scroll wheel in the middle? (tap!)
⚙️ The SCROLL WHEEL helps you go up and down on a page — like turning the pages of a book! Roll it with your finger.
👆 Why do we use a mouse pad? (tap!)
🟫 A MOUSE PAD gives the mouse a smooth, flat surface to slide on. It helps the mouse move accurately and protects the desk!

⌨️ Week 1
Open Microsoft Word! 📄
Microsoft Word is a program where we can type words, make sentences, and create our own documents! Think of it like a magical notebook on the computer. Let's open it!
📖 Steps to Open Word
Opening Microsoft Word 🚀
1
Look at the screen. You will see the Desktop — this is the computer's home screen with small pictures called icons. 🏠
2
Find the Microsoft Word icon — it looks like a blue letter "W". It might be on the desktop or in the taskbar at the bottom. 🔵
3
Double-click the Word icon with your mouse — click twice, fast! 👆👆
4
Wait a moment... Word will open! You'll see a big white page — that's where we will type. A blinking line shows where your text will appear! ▌
✨
The Blinking Cursor!
That little blinking line — ▌ — is called the cursor. It shows exactly where your letters will appear when you start typing. Watch it blink: blink, blink, blink! It's waiting for you to type! ⌨️

🧩 Weeks 2–4
Drag & Drop — Catch and Place! 🤸
Drag and Drop is when you: 1) click and HOLD something, 2) MOVE it somewhere, 3) LET GO to drop it! It's like picking up a toy and putting it in a box.
📖 How to Drag & Drop
The 3 Steps of Drag & Drop
1
Click and HOLD — put your finger on the left mouse button and keep pressing. Don't let go yet! 🤏
2
DRAG — while holding, slide the mouse to where you want to put the item. The item follows your mouse! ➡️
3
DROP — when you are in the right place, let go of the mouse button. The item stays there! 🎯
